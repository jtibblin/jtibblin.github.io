Hi! I'm Julia and I'm currently employed at Laboratoire Parole Langage (Aix-Marseille Université - CNRS) in France, where I'm working on the project [L2SPELL: The impact of orthography on second language word learning and processing](https://anr.fr/en/funded-projects-and-impact/funded-projects/project/funded/project/b2d9d3668f92a3b9fbbf7866072501ef-fef0ae1820/?tx_anrprojects_funded%5Bcontroller%5D=Funded&cHash=5cceed382ac9342270a6db9ad108f261/) (co-PIs: Pauline Welby (LPL, Aix-en-Provence), Elsa Spinelli (LPNC, Grenoble) and Audrey Bürki (Université de Lausanne)).

In January 2026, I defended my [thesis](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/publications/lire-le-langage-inclusif-%C3%A9tudes-exp%C3%A9rimentales-sur-les-repr%C3%A9senta/) in French Linguistics at Lund University, Sweden. Through four empirical studies, I  investigated the effects of French gender-inclusive language on mental representations of gender and language processing in reading with different psycholinguistic methods (sentence-evaluation task, eye tracking).
