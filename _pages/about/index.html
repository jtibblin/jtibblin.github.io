Hi! I’m Julia and I’m currently employed at Laboratoire Parole Langage (Aix-Marseille Université - CNRS) in France, where I’m working on the project L2SPELL: The impact of orthography on second language word learning and processing (PIs: Pauline Welby (LPL, Aix-en-Provence), Elsa Spinelli (LPNC, Grenoble) and Audrey Bürki (Université de Lausanne)).

In January 2026, I defended my thesis in French Linguistics at Lund University, Sweden. Through four empirical studies, I investigated the effects of French gender-inclusive language on mental representations of gender and language processing in reading with different psycholinguistic methods (sentence-evaluation task, eye tracking).
